Free places to learn

  • Microsoft Training and Microsoft Learn: official videos and lessons.
  • YouTube: Kevin Stratvert, Leila Gharani (Excel), and Technology for Teachers and Students.
  • GCFGlobal: beginner lessons with practice exercises.
  • TypingClub or Keybr: free typing practice.

Advice for the learner

  1. Practice every day, even briefly. 45 to 60 minutes daily beats 5 hours once a week.
  2. Use a real computer. Phone-only practice won't build muscle memory. Borrow one, or use a library or cyber cafe.
  3. Build small things you can show. A CV, a budget sheet and a short presentation are proof of skill.
  4. Be honest in interviews. "I'm learning, and here's what I've already built" lands far better than hiding the gap.
  5. Consider a short certificate once you're comfortable, such as Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) prep or a local course.
  6. Don't get discouraged. Basic office skills take weeks, and many people start from zero.
